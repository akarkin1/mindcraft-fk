# Decisions v0.1.4.14 "Minecraft 26.1"

## Before the build: the checks of 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04

| Id | Check | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-1 | Which Minecraft versions the released libraries support (npm, the packages read). | mineflayer 4.39.0 and minecraft-protocol 1.68.0 up to 26.1; minecraft-data 3.119.0 holds 26.1 and no 26.2. | The release targets 26.1. |
| B-2 | Whether the pull requests of PrismarineJS for 26.2 and 26.3 work, built from their branches and run with a plain bot on vanilla 26.2 and 26.3 servers (Java 25). | 26.2: all 10 checks passed, but only with unreleased code of five libraries (also prismarine-chunk #333 and prismarine-physics master); 26.3: the connection holds and the kick of issue #4189 did not happen, but the chunk and physics libraries have no 26.3 code, digging is broken without mineflayer #4146 and the tool speeds come from 26.2 data. | Neither for play. 26.2 when released: a smaller release on top of this one. |
| B-3 | Our 8 patches test-applied on the new versions. | SPEC 4.2: 4 unchanged, 1 at an offset, 1 dropped (upstream), 1 shrunk, 1 rewritten; mineflayer 4.37.1 took the `use_item` rotation and the dig `isInWater` fix upstream, 4.38.0 sends `player_loaded` itself. | As in SPEC 4.2. |

| B-5 | Mojang's notes of 1.21.9, 1.21.10, 1.21.11 and 26.1, read for the features. | PLAN 4b. The chat model does not know them. | Part K, behind `new_things_in_prompt`; the owner decides with a probe of Haiku 5.5 whether the switch stays off. |
| B-6 | The histories of mineflayer 4.34 to 4.39, minecraft-protocol 1.63 to 1.68, prismarine-physics 1.11 and prismarine-chunk 1.40 and 1.41. | B-3's confirmations; a decompression crash caught (1.66.0); a chest-window fix and faster chest transfers (4.39.0); `placeBlock` fails at once on a refusal (4.39.0: our 500 ms timeout patch may be moot); the 26.1 chunks of prismarine-chunk 1.41.0 came from an AI-written pull request. | The engineer of part L decides the place timeout by reading; the gate proves the chunks. |
| B-7 | The open issues of mineflayer that name 1.21.9 to 26.1, read, then tried on a vanilla 26.1 server with the released libraries, a second bot as the hitter and a zombie. | Real: #4167 (no knockback: `entity_velocity` is an `lpVec3` since 1.21.9 and 4.39.0 divides it by 8,000) and #3921 (iron ore 4,550 ms instead of 750 with any pickaxe: the material `incorrect_for_wooden_tool` maps only wooden tools).
